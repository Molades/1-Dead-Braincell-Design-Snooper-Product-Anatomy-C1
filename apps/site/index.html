About

You noticed it works. Now find out why.

Every designer has a folder of screenshots they never open again. The screenshot keeps how something looked, but loses the reason: the type scale, the spacing rhythm, the contrast, the one decision that made it feel right.

DevTools shows you four hundred properties and no opinion. Pixel Peeper picks the part you care about, measures it with code, then writes a short lesson on why it works. Every claim says where it came from, so you always know what is fact and what is interpretation.

Measured

Read from the page by code

Font sizes, weights, colours, spacing, sizes, contrast ratios and rule checks. Exact values, never guessed. If something can't be measured, it says so.

Inferred

The AI's interpretation

Why the choice works, what it's doing for the reader, and how you could borrow it. Clearly labelled as reading, not fact, and always tied to the evidence beneath it.

“The price is 2.5 times the body size, so it's read before the plan name or the features.”

The website stays the star

No side panel. The detective and the lesson float above the page, so the layout you're studying is never squeezed.

Nothing leaves until you ask

Hovering and picking happen on your computer. Only Get lesson sends the selection to be explained.

A lesson before a data dump

One clear idea you can read in under a minute, with the full measurements a tab away when you want them.

Features

From one good detail to a design direction.

Pick, learn, keep, compare, export. Each step works on its own, and together they turn browsing into a reference library.

Pick anything

Hover to preview, click to select. The outline snaps to sensible groups; use ↑ ↓ for bigger or smaller, hold ⌥ for the exact element, or pick the whole visible page.

SectionCardButton164 × 40

Lessons tuned to you

Tell it your level once. Students get terms explained inline; senior designers get terse lessons with the trade-offs first.

Rule checks

Contrast, tap target size, line length and more, checked only where they apply. Passes and fails are shown with the measured value.

Annotated captures

Open any capture full screen with toggleable layers for spacing, type, colour, targets and issues, drawn from real geometry.

A library with sources

Save ideas with a title, note and collection. Find them by site or pattern next week, and jump back to the original page.

Compare two or three

Put references side by side. Differing values are highlighted and a short read-across explains why they feel different.

Build a direction, export it

Borrow type from one reference and colour from another. Contrast is recomputed for the new pairings, then export with every value still labelled and linked to its source.

And a small detective for company

He peeks from the corner of the page, types while a lesson is written, lights up when it's ready and runs off with your saved idea. Quiet sounds play only after you click, and one switch turns them off.

How to

Your first lesson in two minutes.

Seven steps, and only the first two happen once.

  1. 1

    Download and install

    Pixel Peeper isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it yourself. It takes about a minute.

    1. Download the zip and unzip it. Keep the folder somewhere it won't be deleted.
    2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
    3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped pixel-peeper folder.
    4. Open the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar and pin Pixel Peeper.
    Pixel Peeper
  2. 2

    Tell it about you, add a key

    A welcome tab opens after installing. Choose your goals and your level, then paste a Groq or Anthropic API key for written lessons. Every step can be skipped, and measurements work without a key.

  3. 3

    Pick something

    On any website, click the icon or press ⌥ P. The detective appears in the corner. Choose Pick, hover until the outline fits what you like, then click.

    • ↑↓Bigger or smaller target
    • ⌥Hold for the exact element
    • EscStop picking
  4. 4

    Get the lesson

    Press Get lesson (or Enter). The lesson window has three tabs: Lesson for the why, Rules for passes and fails, and Measurements for every value. Rules and measurements are ready before the AI finishes.

  5. 5

    Save the idea

    Choose Save idea. The title is filled in for you; add a note or a collection if you like. Lessons you don't save stay in Recent for seven days.

  6. 6

    Compare in the Library

    Open the Library from the detective's menu. Select two or three ideas and choose Compare to see their values side by side and why they feel different.

  7. 7

    Make a direction and export

    For each category, borrow from A, B or C, or keep your own. Edit the drafted rationale, then export DESIGN.md, tokens.json or CSS to use in your project or hand to a teammate.

Privacy

Your library lives in your browser.

Captures, screenshots, lessons, notes, collections and your API key are stored locally. There's no Pixel Peeper server: we never receive a copy, and uninstalling deletes everything.

Stays on your device

Everything you hover and pick, and everything you save.

Sent on Get lesson

A screenshot of the visible selection, its measured values and rule results, and your chosen level, sent straight from your browser to the AI provider you chose, using your key.

Never sent

The page address, your name or email, or your browsing history.

Never sent to us

Anything at all. Requests go only to Groq or Anthropic, under their own privacy policies.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Pixel Peeper free?

Yes. Picking, measurements, rule checks, the Library, Compare and export are free. Written lessons use your own Groq or Anthropic API key, so any cost is billed by that provider; Groq has a free plan with rate limits.

Do I need an account or an API key?

There's no Pixel Peeper account. For written lessons you need an API key from Groq or Anthropic: paste it once during setup or in Settings. It stays in your browser and is only sent to that provider.

What works without a key?

Everything measured: picking, Rules and Measurements, the annotated captures, saving, Compare's value table and export. Only the written lesson and Compare's read-across need a key.

Why download from GitHub instead of the Chrome Web Store?

Pixel Peeper is in early testing, so builds are shared on GitHub first. Installing with Load unpacked is the standard way to run an extension that isn't in the store. Chrome may remind you that developer-mode extensions are on; that's expected.

How do I update to a new version?

Download the latest zip, replace the files in your pixel-peeper folder with the new ones, then click the reload arrow on Pixel Peeper's card in chrome://extensions. Your saved ideas and key are kept. Removing the extension deletes them.

What's the difference between Measured and Inferred?

Measured values are read from the page by code: exact sizes, colours, spacing and rule results. Inferred is the AI's reading of why those choices work. Each claim carries its label, so you can trust the numbers and judge the opinion.

Does it change the website I'm looking at?

No. Pixel Peeper floats above the page in its own isolated layer, never reserves width, and doesn't load its fonts or styles into the site. Close it and the page is exactly as it was.

Does it work on every website?

It works on ordinary websites. Chrome doesn't let extensions run on its own pages (like settings or the Web Store) or some protected pages; the icon shows a short note when that happens. After installing, reload any tabs that were already open.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome on desktop today. Other Chromium browsers that install Chrome extensions, such as Edge, Arc and Brave, are likely to work but aren't tested yet.

Can I use the exports in Figma or my codebase?

Yes. tokens.json imports into token plugins, styles.css gives you custom properties, and DESIGN.md is a readable direction for teammates or AI coding tools. Every value keeps its source link.

Is it ok to learn from other people's websites?

Pixel Peeper is for studying how design decisions work, the way you'd sketch from a book. It keeps the source with every idea so you can credit and revisit it. Borrow principles and values, not whole designs.

How do I turn the sounds off?

Open the detective's menu and switch sound off; it's remembered. Sounds never play until you've clicked something of ours, and never in a hidden tab.
