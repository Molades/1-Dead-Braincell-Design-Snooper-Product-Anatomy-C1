# Pixel Peeper privacy policy (draft)

> Draft for review before publishing. Not legal advice; have it checked before release.

**Last updated:** _date of publication_

## What stays on your device

Everything you save stays in your browser: captures, screenshots, lessons, notes, collections and
comparisons. We do not have a copy and cannot see it. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

## What is sent, and when

Nothing is sent while you hover over or pick parts of a page. When you choose **Get lesson**, the
extension sends to Pixel Peeper's server:

- a screenshot of the part of the page you selected (only what is visible on screen),
- measured style values of that selection (fonts, colors, spacing, sizes) and rule-check results,
- the level you chose during setup (student, practising, senior).

The page address (URL) is not sent. When you choose **Explain the differences** or **Draft
rationale** in Compare, the measured values and earlier lessons of those ideas are sent as text,
without screenshots.

## What our server does with it

Our server forwards the request to an AI model provider (Groq) to write the lesson, returns the
answer to your browser, and does not store screenshots or lesson content. Groq processes the
request under its own terms and privacy policy.

To enforce the daily allowance, the server keeps a count of lessons per anonymous install ID for
about two days, and a count of new installs per network address for one day. The install ID is a
random identifier created by the extension; it is not linked to your name, email or browsing
history.

## What we do not do

No accounts, no advertising, no selling data, no tracking of the sites you visit.
